Deep Pre-Buffer (Load Upcoming Minutes) Unlocks YouTube 15s limit to continuously download the next minutes at full connection speed.
Parallel RAM Pre-Fetch (64MB sidx Cache) Pre-fetches upcoming segment byte-ranges in RAM before the player requests them.
Initial Headstart Cushion (Track Before Train) Builds a solid initial 4K buffer cushion at second zero before releasing playback.
Block AV1 Codec (Force Hardware VP9) Prevents CPU software AV1 decoding on graphics cards without native hardware support.
Block VP9 10-bit HDR (Force 4K SDR) Prevents shader tonemapping overload on 4 GB VRAM GPUs while keeping smooth 2160p.
Limit Streams to 30fps (Block 60fps) Halves decoded frames per second on high-FPS videos to relieve entry-level GPUs.
MSE Buffer & Micro-Gap Stabilizer Bridges millisecond timestamp gaps between SourceBuffer segments to prevent stalls.
Disable Ambient Mode (GPU Saver) Removes YouTube blurred lighting canvas to free VRAM and GPU fill-rate.
Severe Frame Drop Protection Automatically steps down from 4K to 2K if frame drop rate exceeds 18%.
Sync Local Telemetry (Node.js / Ruby) Optional for developers: sends logs to localhost:3456 with zero end-user impact.

Open Source Architecture v2.1.0

My Quality 4k unlocks YouTube SABR 35 MB (16s) internal buffer cap to 512 MB, pre-buffering 1, 2, or 3 full minutes of 4K (2160p) video while enforcing hardware VP9 SDR decoding and zero-reload live updates.
