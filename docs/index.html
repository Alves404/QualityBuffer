Unlock Multi-Minute 4K YouTube Pre-Buffering & V12 HLS/MP4 Downloading

My Quality removes YouTube's hardcoded 35 MB (16-second) 4K SABR buffer cap to pre-load 1, 2, or 3 upcoming minutes of 2160p video while enforcing hardware-accelerated VP9 SDR decoding and integrating a 12-worker HLS (M3U8 / AES-128) & MP4 web video downloader.

1. Core Engineering Architecture (v2.2.1)

512 MB SABR & Policy Unlocker

YouTube's internal base.js player caps 4K video buffers at 36,700,160 bytes (35 MB = 16.3s) and enforces a 15-second server-driven targetVideoReadaheadMs. My Quality intercepts g.vWa policy objects and the SABR request coordinator to expand the video budget to 512 MB and continuously stream up to 60s, 120s, or 180s ahead. (Side-effect: Sub-4K streams like 1080p and 720p buffer their target minutes almost instantaneously).

Hardware Codec Enforcement (VP9 SDR)

Intercepts MediaSource.isTypeSupported and navigator.mediaCapabilities.decodingInfo to block software-heavy AV1 (av01) and 10-bit VP9 HDR (vp09.02), certifying 8-bit VP9 Profile 0 for smooth GPU hardware decoding.

Zero-Reload Live Apply & Switch Indicator

Features a switch-style Green/Red status indicator beside the Aplicar Agora button (Green = Applied, Red = Unapplied Changes) that hot-applies resolution, SABR horizon, and codec changes live without reloading the YouTube page.

MSE Micro-Gap Stabilizer & Zero-Overhead Multi-Language UI

Automatically bridges millisecond timestamp gaps in video.buffered to prevent infinite spinner stalls, while providing a zero-overhead multi-language interface (English default + automatic browser detection for EN, PT, and ES).

2. Installation & Usage

  1. Download My-Quality-v2.2.1.zip from GitHub Releases and extract the archive to a permanent folder.
  2. Open Google Chrome, Brave, or Edge and navigate to chrome://extensions/.
  3. Enable Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked in the top-left corner and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  5. Open any 4K YouTube video or web video page, click the Video Player extension icon, and switch between Controls and Downloader.

3. V12 Async Warm-up & 12-Worker HLS/MP4 Downloader

Standalone & Zero-Telemetry Architecture: The extension operates 100% locally in your browser with zero telemetry servers. The integrated Downloader tab captures standard HLS (.m3u8 with AES-128 encryption) and direct .mp4/.webm streams on web pages, automatically selecting the highest-bandwidth stream, pre-warming AES keys in WebCrypto, and downloading with 12 concurrent workers while yielding the event loop so active 4K playback never drops frames.
